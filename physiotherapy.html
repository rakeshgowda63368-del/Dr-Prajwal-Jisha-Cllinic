Restoring Strength, Mobility & Function

At Jisha Prime Orthopaedic & Spine Care, we believe that expert surgery is only the first step toward full recovery. Our specialized Rehabilitation and Physiotherapy department operates hand-in-hand with Dr. Prajwal Narayan to provide seamless, evidence-based physical therapy.

Whether you are recovering from a complex spine procedure, a joint replacement, or a sports injury, our expert physical therapists design customized protocols tailored to your specific biomechanical needs and lifestyle goals.
