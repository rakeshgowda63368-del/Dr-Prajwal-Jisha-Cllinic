Endoscopic Spine Surgery Advanced minimally invasive procedures for disc herniation, spinal stenosis, and complex deformities. Including endoscopic spine surgery and motion-preserving disc replacement for faster recovery.

Sports Injury Expert management of ligament tears, ACL reconstruction, meniscus repairs, and shoulder arthroscopy. Tailored recovery programs for athletes to return to peak performance safely.

Arthritis Care Comprehensive treatment for osteoarthritis and rheumatoid arthritis. Utilizing intra-articular injections, biologics, and customized rehabilitation to reduce pain and preserve joint function.

Rehabilitation
